# Watering Can

A houseplant care app where each plant is a little animated pal. Pals droop when they're thirsty, wilt when you're late, and perk back up when you water them.

## Features

- **Garden:** your plants on a shelf, each drawn as an animated pal: happy, thirsty or wilting.
- **Add a plant:** from a photo, which Claude identifies, with a care plan and health check. Or pick from a catalog of 52 plants, including many philodendrons and dracaenas.
- **Watering for pot size:** the schedule adjusts for pot diameter, pot type and plant size, and tells you how much water to give.
- **Still moist:** pushes the next watering back when the soil is still damp deep down. This matters most in big 10–12″ pots.
- **Photo proof and streaks:** snap a photo when you water to log it and build an on-time streak.
- **Health check:** Claude looks at a photo for pests, disease and watering problems, and lists what to keep on hand.
- **Reminders:** saves a calendar file with a repeating reminder for each plant.

## Running it

It's a single file, `index.html`, with no build step. Open it in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repo.

Outside Claude, plants are saved in your browser only. Photo ID, health checks and the calendar button need the Claude version of the app, which uses your Claude account.
